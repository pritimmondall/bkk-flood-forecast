### Read with these four caveats

1. **Typical warning time is about 15 minutes** — one measurement step.
   This is a detection system today, not yet a warning system.
2. **The model beats a simple rain threshold by roughly one point** of
   recall on dry roads. The limit is the data available, not the method.
3. **Depth estimates are not published.** The prediction intervals fail
   their coverage check (see notebook 12).
4. **Never compare scores between years.** 2024 had a fifth as many
   floods as 2022; PR-AUC moves with that, skill does not.
